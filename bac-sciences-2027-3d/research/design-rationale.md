# Design rationale — creative directions and the final art direction

## 1. Brief in one paragraph
A graduation collection for the **Baccalauréat Sciences 2027** class of the Lycée Hannibal in Tébourba (Tunisia): 3 T-shirts, 3 hoodies, 2 jackets, 2 vests, 2 scarves, plus cap, beanie, tote bag, embroidered patch and woven label. Streetwear silhouette, serious science content, a local story (Hannibal, Surus, Tébourba / Thuburbo Minus), and a palette that reads at a distance. Final designs are **3D** (live-looking, rotatable), not flat artwork.

## 2. Four creative directions that were explored

| # | Direction | Idea | Strengths | Weaknesses | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Laboratoire Rose** | Soft dusty-rose fleece, embroidered script, woven crests; “lab coat meets letterman”. Inspired by ref 1. | Warm, photogenic, unusual for a science class, reads as premium. | Alone it can feel too soft/pastel for boys’ sizes. | **Kept as the hero** (H1) and as the tonal link across the range. |
| B | **Cosmos Nuit** | Deep navy technical fleece, cosmic print, orbit geometry. Inspired by ref 2. | Dramatic, universal, great for the back print; makes “physics” visible. | Dark garments hide embroidery detail; risk of looking like a generic space brand. | **Kept** (H3, S1) — made specific by the Surus atom-ear crest and the orbit repeat. |
| C | **Collège Gris** | Grey marl collegiate hoodie with an illustrative collage. Inspired by ref 3. | Most “school” of all; the collage lets every science (Physique, Chimie, SVT) have a badge. | Collage needs careful composition or it turns into clutter. | **Kept** (H2, J1/V1 back arch). Collage re-drawn on a strict grid. |
| D | **Tableau Périodique** | Typographic: element tiles spelling a word. Inspired by ref 6. | Instantly legible, internationally scientific, cheap to print, a strong *word* (B·Ac·Sc·I = BAC SCI). | Needs scientific accuracy (it is correct) and good tile colours on every ground. | **Kept** (T2, H1 back, patches). |
| E | *Hannibal Alpin* (explored, not selected) | Alpine peaks (Λ) + elephant in a mountain scene. | Strong local story. | Illustrative scenes are hard to embroider; competes with the science language. | Reduced to a **motif**: the “A” of HΛNNIBΛL is a Λ (peak) in the wordmark. |

## 3. Final art direction — “Orbit-Λ”
One idea ties the 17 pieces together: **an atom orbit is a mountain pass** — science (orbit, nucleus, tiles, DNA, molecule) wearing Hannibal’s story (Λ peaks, Surus the elephant, the crossing of the unknown).

* **Hero pieces**: H1 ROSE ORBITE (ref 1), H3 NUIT COSMIQUE (ref 2), H2 GRIS COLLÈGE (ref 3).
* **Graphics**: tiles B·Ac·Sc·I (ref 6), DNA (ref 4), caffeine molecule (ref 5), cosmic orbit print (ref 2), Punnett square, collage.
* **Identity**: wordmark **HΛNNIBΛL**, circular **crest (Surus with an atom-ring ear)**, **2027** with an orbit zero, woven labels, Λ peak line.

| Tunisian red | #C8102E | **controlled accent only** — reserved for very small details (not used on the first release) |

### Typography
* Wordmark & headings: **Archivo** (wide, heavy) with the A drawn as a Λ.
* Scientific annotations: **JetBrains Mono**.
* Limited-edition script: **Great Vibes** (“Bac Sciences”).
* Varsity: **Graduate**.
* Arabic: **Aref Ruqaa** (calligraphic « علوم ») and **Noto Kufi Arabic** (motto), shaped with HarfBuzz — to be reviewed by a native reader before production.
* French accents are real glyphs: LYCÉE, TÉBOURBA, MATIÈRE.

### Placement system (applied on every item)
Left-chest small embroidered logo · optional right-chest patch · sleeve script/“2027” · large statement graphic on the back · hood motto · woven label at the hem · scarf repeat with contrasting ends · cap monogram + back “2027” · tote central motif.

## 4. Why these 17 pieces
* **3 tees** cover three price/effort levels: minimal (T1: embroidery + one-colour print), bold graphic (T2: 4-colour tiles), illustration (T3: molecule + Punnett).
* **3 hoodies**: the hero with hardware (H1), the classic pullover (H2) and the technical layer (H3).
* **2 jackets + 2 vests**: varsity (wool/leather — the classic graduation garment) and light technical (coach / puffer) so the range works in all seasons.
* **2 scarves**: one loud (orbit all-over), one quiet (typographic border).
* **Complementary**: cap, beanie, tote, patch, label — cheap items that extend the graphic system and fund the rest.

## 5. What we deliberately did **not** do
* No copied logos or graphics from the references.
* No AI-generated lettering on garments: all type is real, outlined font glyphs.
* No unverifiable quotation: the “energy” line is unattributed.
* No claim of production readiness for the Arabic lines until reviewed.
